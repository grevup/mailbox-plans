All ¼″ plywood. For the working lid: don't glue the roof — attach it with 2 small hinges (or a duct-tape hinge) along the BACK edge. For the flag: pivot on a #8 bolt + washers + nylon lock nut, snugged until the arm swings stiffly; a small brad behind the raised arm parks it upright.
tip: open the lid, then drop a letter

Sheet Cutting Diagram

One 24″ × 24″ sheet = one mailbox. First rip into three strips (7½″, 6″, and the ~10″ leftover), then crosscut each strip. Leave about ⅛″ between pieces for the saw blade.

Cut order: ① rip at 7½″  →  ② rip the rest at 6″  →  ③ crosscut strip A at 10″, 7″, 6″  →  ④ crosscut strip B into two 10″ pieces  →  ⑤ cut the roof 9″×8″ from strip C  →  ⑥ angle cuts on the sides (below).

Side angle cut: on each 6″×10″ side piece, mark 8½″ up the front edge and draw a line to the top back corner. Cut the triangle off. Flip one piece over (or stack and cut both at once) so the two sides mirror each other.
